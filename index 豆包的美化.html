About Us

Shenzhen Covien Technology Co., Ltd., founded in 2022, is a commercial photo booth manufacturer with integrated in-house metal and woodworking production, including laser cutting, CNC machining, welding, painting, and assembly lines. We hold 27+ design and utility patents and support full customization across appearance, function, and packaging. Every unit undergoes 100% power-on and functional testing before shipment. We provide a one-year standard warranty, 7×12 remote technical support, and one-stop procurement for printers and cameras.

FAQs

Why choose Covien?

We specialize in commercial Photo Booth equipment manufacturing, providing professional solutions for Photo Booth Rental Companies, Event Companies, and distributors. With our own design, production, and quality control capabilities, we provide stable products, flexible customization, and reliable support.

How do you guarantee quality?

All products undergo strict quality control, including material inspection, production monitoring, and final function testing. Every Photo Booth is tested for structure, electrical safety, and operation performance before shipment.

Do you support OEM customization?

Yes, we support customization including color, logo, appearance, screen, printer configuration, packaging, and functions. We help customers develop exclusive Photo Booth products for their local markets.

How do you ship the Photo Booth?

All Photo Booths are shipped with professional flight cases at no extra cost. The flight case provides strong protection and reduces damage risk during international shipping.

What if I need installation support?

We provide installation videos, manuals, and online technical support. The modular design allows easy setup, and our team provides continuous after-sales assistance.

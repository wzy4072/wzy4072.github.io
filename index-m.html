About Us

Your Photo Booth Should Work. Every Single Time.

Shenzhen Covien Technology Co., Ltd., founded in 2022, is a commercial photo booth manufacturer with integrated in-house metal and woodworking production, including laser cutting, CNC machining, welding, painting, and assembly lines.

We hold 27+ design and utility patents and support full customization across appearance, function, and packaging. Every unit undergoes 100% power-on and functional testing before shipment.

We provide a one-year standard warranty, 7×12 remote technical support, and one-stop procurement for printers and cameras.

What is Our Goal?

  • In-house production from sheet metal to finished booth — reliable lead times, no excuses
  • Customization for iPad/camera models, colors, and flight cases with handles & wheels
  • 100% power-on test before shipment — plug and play on site
  • One-year warranty, free replacement for damaged parts
  • One-stop procurement — we source printers & cameras for you
